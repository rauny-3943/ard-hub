Read the score

Score = number of searches that found the paper + 2 for each corpus paper it cites. Papers that cite the corpus are usually the most relevant; a score of 1 is often noise.

Keep or reject

Click Keep or Reject under each paper (you can set its theme and region), then Send decisions at the bottom of the screen. GitHub opens a pre-filled issue: click Create. Kept papers join the map within a few minutes; rejected ones never come back.
